# 0003. The lead session implements; there is no implementing subagent

Date: 2026-09-22

## Context
On Campfire, a Fable lead planned while one Opus 5 subagent wrote the code, and that habit is saved in session memory
for other projects. Here the lead is Opus 5.5, and the owner wants to see its own work.

## Decision
The lead plans, edits and reviews directly. Step 1 of `docs/workflow/03-implement.md` (one implementing agent,
rotated at about 30% of its window) does not apply, and `docs/workflow/project.md` says so.

## Consequences
The lead still reads its own work as a diff before phase 4. Search and research subagents are not covered.
